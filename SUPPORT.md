# Getting help

eCash Card is the home of two experimental open source projects. Help comes from their maintainers on a best-effort
basis.

- **Cashu Card:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/cashucard/cashucard/discussions) on cashucard/cashucard.
- **Fedimint Card:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/fedimintcard/fedimintcard/discussions) on fedimintcard/fedimintcard.
- **The ecashcard.org site:** open an issue in [ecashcard/ecashcard.org](https://github.com/ecashcard/ecashcard.org/issues).
- **Vulnerabilities:** report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
- **Anything else:** [hi@ecashcard.org](mailto:hi@ecashcard.org).
